# Kinderspielstadt Los Ämmerles e.V.

Non-profit association in Ammerbuch — we are digitalising the [Kinderspielstadt](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinderstadt) “Los Ämmerles”.

In our summer camp, children experience everyday life and society through realistic roles — baker, bank clerk, carpenter, and many more. Together with our client apps, **children and staff** use this software during the camp. All code here is open source under the [MIT license](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT).
